About Me

Who am I?

I am a transportation and logistics specialist pursuing a PhD at AGH University of Krakow, focused on the digital transformation of logistics. My doctoral research develops a framework for LLM-assisted logistics problem structuring and optimization: using language models to extract objectives and constraints from operational problem descriptions and turn them into solvable mathematical models.

Before my PhD, I worked as a lecturer and acting head of a civil engineering department, a graduate engineer supporting logistics planning and road construction, and a research assistant studying public transport safety. I am a UNESCO/Poland Fellowship alumnus (2023A) and completed the Technology in Supply Chain Bootcamp at MIT CTL in 2025.
